Help
  • Gravity is the bot's gravity belief; Local corrections adds the observed residual to the fitted planets (off shows the planets alone). Evidence marks where recorded paths support a residual. Error is belief minus the disclosed map and exists only when the capture has one.
  • Model disagreement is how far the gravity of the no-source model and the validated field-only source model differ, weighted by held-out improvement; it exists only while a validated source does, and is a heuristic, not a probability. It says nothing about space where neither model has evidence.
  • Dark cells have no value for the layer (not zero gravity). Uncertainty comes from the fit covariance and ignores omitted planets and model error.
  • Disclosed planets are the game's own map, for diagnosis only. A disclosed planet is a close match when an estimate lies within 10 units and 5 units of radius; center off / size off exceed one threshold; missing has no estimate within 80 units. Pairs are one-to-one, closest first.
  • Spawn samples (blue squares) are positions where players were observed to appear in the current map epoch, as recorded by the bot. They are samples, not a complete set.
  • Contacts are path endpoints the estimator accepted as surface hits, not every collision. The pink diamond is a field-only source: a gravity explanation, never collision geometry.
  • Shot review re-simulates the decision's chosen shot under the recorded belief and compares it with the shot that actually followed. Outcomes are read off the observed endpoint and are inferences, not server reports. The green timeline line is each decision's belief scored against the next 5 observed shots (median path error), which needs no disclosed map. Repairs change one planet of the belief to the disclosed one and re-simulate: a sensitivity, not proof of cause.
  • Faded dots are where a player stood before it moved. Scenario shots are labelled from what the scenario generator recorded: training shots build the baseline information, the trial shot is the policy's shot (muted versus bold lines).
  • “Map events pending” in the decision panel means the snapshot may lag the latest observations.
