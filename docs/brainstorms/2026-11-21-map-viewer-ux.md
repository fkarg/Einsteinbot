### Degradation experiment (2026-11-21; user: 'degradation experiment next sounds good. consider subagents.')

Correction after review (cross-model + two subagent reviews): the first displacement ladder moved the planet TOWARD the path (offset sign), so the earlier '3.3 units of miss per unit' figure was measured in the wrong direction. Fixed and re-measured (capture 44 decision #243, observed): moving the nearest planet (surface 36 from the path) 1 unit away keeps the perfect shot a hit; 2 units -> closest approach 7.8; 5 -> 12.9; 10 -> 22.5; 20 -> 39.5. Dropping a planet that passes 36-72 units from the path makes the perfect shot miss by 40-234. Planet radius -1/+1 -> 20-23; every planet off by ~1 unit per axis -> 12-53. So a hit tolerates roughly 1 unit of error in a nearby planet (target radius 4); capture 44's median matched center error is ~3.
